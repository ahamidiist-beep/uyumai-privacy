UyumAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

UyumAI ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use UyumAI.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, UyumAI may collect:

  • Account information such as name and email address.
  • Profile information and compatibility questionnaire responses.
  • Voice profile information and voice recordings when you use voice-related features.
  • Audio recordings of conversations when you intentionally start a compatibility analysis.
  • Compatibility analysis results and related information.
  • Purchase and transaction information associated with in-app purchases.
  • Technical information necessary for authentication, security, and operation of the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Provide compatibility profiles and compatibility analysis.
  • Process voice and conversation data when you request analysis.
  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions.
  • Process in-app purchases and credits.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Improve the reliability and security of the App.
  • Provide customer support.

3. Voice and Conversation Data

UyumAI may process voice recordings and conversation audio when you explicitly use the recording and analysis features. The purpose of this processing is to identify the user's voice where applicable and to perform compatibility analysis.

You should only record conversations when you have the necessary permission and legal right to do so. You are responsible for complying with applicable laws and obtaining any required consent from other participants.

4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

UyumAI uses artificial intelligence technologies to process information provided by users and generate compatibility-related results. AI-generated results are intended for informational and entertainment purposes and should not be considered professional medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice.

5. Purchases

UyumAI offers optional in-app purchases. Payments made through the App Store are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your complete payment card number through UyumAI. Purchase and transaction information may be processed to verify purchases, provide purchased credits, prevent duplicate transactions, and maintain purchase records.

6. Third-Party Services

UyumAI may use trusted third-party infrastructure and service providers necessary to operate the App, including cloud hosting, database services, authentication services, artificial intelligence processing services, and Apple's App Store services. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and according to their applicable privacy policies.

7. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. Authentication credentials and session information are protected using appropriate security mechanisms.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their UyumAI account and associated personal information through the available account deletion functionality or by contacting us. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and transaction-record purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

UyumAI is not intended for children under the applicable minimum age required by law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

11. International Data Processing

Depending on the services used and the location of our service providers, your information may be processed or stored in countries other than your country of residence. Appropriate safeguards will be applied where required by applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact UyumAI through the contact information provided within the App or its official support channels.
